## The problem

Wholesale diamond trading ran on WhatsApp, email and spreadsheets. A buyer wanted a 1.5ct round, VS1, no BGM, eye-clean. Someone checked several supplier lists by hand, replied with options, negotiated, and re-keyed the order.

Every step was manual, every price was a point-in-time guess, and cross-border pricing errors were routine — because the person quoting was mentally converting currency and duty on the fly.

## What I built

[thediamondport.com](https://thediamondport.com) — a marketplace that aggregates **multiple supplier inventories behind one search**, built in **Next.js and Tailwind**.

**Aggregation.** Supplier stock arrives as API feeds in incompatible shapes and refresh cadences. The ingestion layer normalises them into one schema, so a buyer filters across 2,000+ SKUs without knowing or caring which supplier holds the stone.

**Search that matches how the trade actually thinks.** Not generic e-commerce facets — the filters are the trade's own vocabulary: cut, colour, clarity, certificate, and quality parameters like *no BGM* and *eye clean* that do not exist in any standard product schema.

**Evidence per stone.** Video, images and the grading certificate attached to each listing, because nobody buys a diamond from a text row.

**Distribution beyond the site.** An **API feed** and **white-label iframe** so partner jewellers can surface the same inventory inside their own sites — which is how a marketplace grows without growing its own traffic.

## Scale and reach

2,000+ SKUs from multiple suppliers, operating across the US, Australia, the UK, India, Belgium and Hong Kong — six markets, each with its own pricing, currency and logistics reality.

## What was hard

**Feed volatility.** Stones sell. A listing that was valid ninety seconds ago may be gone, and showing a buyer something they cannot have is worse than showing them less. Cache lifetimes had to be short enough to stay honest and long enough that the catalogue stayed fast.

**No canonical schema.** Every supplier describes a diamond slightly differently. Normalisation is where most of the real work went, and it is never finished — each new supplier brings a new dialect.

## Stack

Next.js · React · Tailwind CSS · Node.js · REST and GraphQL APIs · supplier feed ingestion · iframe embedding
