"You need Plus for checkout customisation" is the answer you will usually get. It is close enough to be useful and wrong often enough to cost you a project. The line is more specific than that.

## Shopify Functions: available on every plan — with one catch

Functions run on all plans **when they are distributed through a public app** from the Shopify App Store. Discount, Cart and Checkout Validation, Delivery Customization, Payment Customization, Cart Transform and Pickup Point are all live for non-Plus merchants that way.

The catch is the distribution model, not the plan:

> **Custom apps containing Function APIs are Plus-only. Public App Store apps containing the same Functions are not.**

So a merchant on Shopify or Advanced can absolutely get Function-powered behaviour. They just cannot get it from a one-off custom app built only for them — it has to come through a published app.

That single distinction explains most of the confusion in this area.

## Checkout UI extensions: this is where Plus actually matters

UI extensions on the **core checkout steps — Information, Shipping, Payment — require Plus.** Full stop. So does the complete Checkout Branding API and per-market checkout configuration.

What a non-Plus merchant *does* get:

- app blocks on the **Thank You** and **Order Status** pages
- colours and fonts through the checkout editor

That is a real capability, and it covers a surprising amount of post-purchase work. It is not the same as owning the payment step.

## The deadline nobody is talking about

Plus stores had to migrate their Thank You and Order Status pages by **28 August 2025**. That one got attention.

The non-Plus deadline is **26 August 2026**.

If you are reading this when it was published, that is three weeks away, and it applies to the far larger population of stores. Any store still relying on legacy `additional_scripts` on those pages is on a clock. The migration path is app blocks and Functions — which loops back to the first section: the public-app route is how a non-Plus store gets there.

## Why the precision is worth having

It is the difference between "we'd need to upgrade you to Plus" and "we can do this on your current plan, through a published app, and here's the deadline you should be planning around anyway."

One of those answers loses the project. The other sounds like someone who has actually shipped on the platform.
